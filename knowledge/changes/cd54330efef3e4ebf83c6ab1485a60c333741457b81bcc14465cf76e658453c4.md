## Summary
New TimeUtils class and module-level time_utils singleton. get_current_time optionally fetches time from WorldTimeAPI or TimezoneDB (demo key) over plain HTTP with a 5-minute cache, falls back to the system clock, and forces a hard-coded BASE_YEAR of 2025 when the year is earlier. Provides Chinese weekday formatting in standard/short/sheet_name styles, parse_shipping_date for YYYY-MM-DD, MM-DD (year = 2025, rolled to next year if already past) and relative phrases such as 今天/明天/後天/N天後, a week-range helper, and validate_shipping_date rejecting past dates and dates more than a year out.
