# c032ccc 聊天紀錄解析加上 emoji 前處理

## Summary
Adds an emoji/zero-width-character sanitizer and applies it in the chat-log importer at two points: the whole chat log is stripped before being sent to the LLM (logging and recording the removed count in trace metadata), and every field coming back from the LLM is cleaned via _text(), which now delegates to clean_field().

## Why
LINE chat logs are full of emoji that the model copies into name/address fields, producing dirty sheet data and breaking the receiver/phone/date duplicate check. Stripping before the LLM also saves tokens.

## Risks
The regex covers broad Unicode ranges (e.g. U+2600-27BF, U+2190-21FF), which could remove legitimate symbols such as arrows or check marks that appear in messages; keycap digits keep their number. Whitespace collapsing may alter spacing inside addresses.

## Files
- `src/services/chat_log_importer.py` (M, +15/-4): Strips emoji from the chat log before parsing, adds emoji_chars_removed to the trace metadata, and makes _text() use clean_field() so every LLM output field is sanitized.
