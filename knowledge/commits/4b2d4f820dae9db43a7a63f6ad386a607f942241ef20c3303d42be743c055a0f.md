# 22fce83 Add the 雙雙對對 item to the price list at a fixed price of $3,400

## Summary
Adds a new product 雙雙對對 (a fixed-combination gift set) to PriceCalculator's price table at a flat unit price of 3400 with no 18A/20A spec. Product-name parsing short-circuits to {type: 雙雙對對, spec: 雙雙對對} whenever the name contains that label, and price calculation multiplies the flat price by quantity with a '$price x qty' detail string.

## Why
A new sellable item needed to be priced; only the price calculator is updated here, with form options and parsing following in 48cea3e.

## Risks
The substring check `'雙雙對對' in name` runs before spec matching, so any product string containing that label is priced as the flat item regardless of other content. Other parts of the pipeline (form options, chat-log parser, shipping summary) did not yet recognize the item at this point.
