# 5a84eaf 出貨統計的數量改用阿拉伯數字顯示

## Summary
Changes the shipping-summary display so quantities are shown with Arabic numerals (e.g. '1盒*2個地址' instead of '一盒*2個地址'); the _INT_TO_CHINESE table is removed and SummaryLine.text formats the integer directly. Docstrings in the CLI and organizer plus the CONTEXT.md example are updated to match.

## Why
Display preference stated in the message; parsing is unchanged and still accepts both Chinese and Arabic numerals from the form options.

## Risks
Only output formatting changes; anything matching the old Chinese-numeral summary text would need updating.
