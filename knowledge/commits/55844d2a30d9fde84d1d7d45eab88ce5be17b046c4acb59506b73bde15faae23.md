# 9c1f24c 移除版控裡的自簽 TLS 憑證與私鑰

## Summary
Adds `ssl/*.pem` to .gitignore, with a comment that the self-signed TLS certificate and key are generated locally by ./setup_ssl.sh and must never be committed.

## Why
setup_ssl.sh writes ssl/cert.pem and ssl/key.pem into the working tree; ignoring them keeps a private key from ever entering version control.
