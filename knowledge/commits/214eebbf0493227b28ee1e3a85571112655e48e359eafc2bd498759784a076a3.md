# da5f2c8 出貨統計相容「雙雙對對*數量」手打短文字

## Summary
Makes the shipping summary also recognize the hand-typed shorthand '雙雙對對*N' (label, asterisk, digits) in addition to the formal option string. A new _SHUANGSHUANG_SHORTHAND_PATTERN is checked in _parse_option after the formal pattern and yields the same (雙雙對對, '', N, 組) tuple.

## Why
Until the Google Form gets the dropdown option, staff type the shorthand directly into the sheet, which the summary flagged as unparsed and left out of the totals (commit message).

## Risks
Low; the shorthand regex requires the whole cell to match, so it will not catch variants with extra text or full-width asterisks. Once the formal option exists, both formats count toward the same key.
